Source pages describe Pune's Mutha River. Dates come from the file-page/camera metadata, not independently verified camera clocks. Exact time and time zone are not asserted. Source metadata for the latter two photographs names editing software; no camera-original or unaltered-scene claim is made. Precise locations are not inferred from the photos. The Sambhaji source supplies coordinates, but they are not copied into a new observation.

The application stores photographer, title, source URL, licence URL, source date, thumbnail description and SHA-256 alongside each explicit photo review. Review authors write their own words and acknowledge uncertainty. References are excluded from current site weather and the field-observation atlas. Reuse of each photograph remains subject to its linked licence; photographer endorsement is not implied.
