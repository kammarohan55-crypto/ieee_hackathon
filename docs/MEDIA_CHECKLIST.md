# Photos for the AquaLens hackathon demo

You can use a phone. Photos alone are enough; no hardware or water-testing kit is required.

## Minimum useful set: one real stream, three original photos

| File | Capture | What it contributes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01-wide.jpg` | Landscape view of the stream and its banks, with a recognizable bridge, path or landmark | Site context and a repeatable viewpoint |
| `02-water.jpg` | Closer view of the water surface, taken from a safe path or bank | Visible appearance, floating material or other details you actually saw |
| `03-bank.jpg` | Riverbank, vegetation, litter or habitat features | Environmental and One Health context |

Send the original JPEG, PNG or WebP files, each under 25 MB. Avoid filters, AI edits, screenshots of photographs, and screenshots of social media. The app records upload time separately from the observation time that you enter; it does not claim to extract or verify EXIF capture time.

For each observation, include:

- Stream name or a clear nearby landmark, city and country.
- Actual date and time of the visit, with your time zone.
- A short description in your own words of what you directly saw.
- Coordinates if you know them or choose to use device location; otherwise leave them blank.
- Whether you took the images, or who owns them and what permission you have to use them.

Keep pH, temperature, conductivity and turbidity blank unless you actually measured them with an instrument. Images do not provide those readings. Wildlife not seen during one visit does not establish absence.

## Optional additions that strengthen the demo

- Another photo from the **same viewpoint on another actual visit**, with its real time, enables an honest comparison. Do not invent a date or describe different viewpoints as a measured change.
- A stable **5–10 second MP4/WebM clip** can show the visible scene. Uploads support up to 15 seconds and 25 MB; in-app recording stops at 10 seconds. The app retains video and checks its first frame; it does not perform AI video analysis.
- A second nearby site is useful for showing the supplied-coordinate map, but not required.

Stay on a safe, publicly accessible path. Do not enter the water or approach an outfall to improve a photo. Avoid identifiable faces and private property details.

## How the images appear in the project

Use Field notebook → Upload photo or clip. Add the actual place/time and original note, complete clarification, and confirm. The files remain in this browser unless you explicitly request photo AI or export them. The Review desk retains originals and digests. Field kit → Download field pack can carry the files to another browser.

Publicly sourced photos may be used only with their actual source, date and permission. They are not your field visit, current water measurements or a live monitoring feed. This release does not seed borrowed photographs into citizen records.
