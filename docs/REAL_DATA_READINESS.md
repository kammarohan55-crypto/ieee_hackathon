## Optional evidence that strengthens specific features

| Optional supply | Record accurately | Feature and limit |
|---|---|---|
| GPS | WGS84 latitude/longitude, device/manual source and available accuracy or uncertainty. Leave blank if unknown or unsuitable to publish. | Geographic map and explicit current modeled-weather lookup. Manual coordinates have no accuracy input; retain uncertainty in the note/sidecar. Weather is not a water measurement or past-visit weather. |

| One Health context | Directly observed bank type, wildlife seen/not seen, human activity and unknowns. | Optional contextual fields. One visit without seeing wildlife does not establish absence; appearance does not establish safety, pathogens, pollution identity or ecological status. |

## Human review and evidence of usefulness

- For the demonstration, a real person must inspect the originals, answer only what they know, explicitly confirm the citizen entry, and record an actual review reason. The same participant may demonstrate both roles if disclosed. Keep disagreements and unknowns; do not overwrite the original words with AI suggestions.
- For stronger evaluation, supply a review from another person: name or approved pseudonym, actual role/qualification (including no specialist qualification), consent to quote/credit, date, records inspected, method, judgments and limitations. Expert evaluation is optional, and no expert credential is an official prerequisite verified here. Record reviewer context in the review note/sidecar because the app uses an unauthenticated **demo reviewer** role.
- Any reported user/expert feedback needs the actual comments and evaluation method. Do not turn a few reviews or the authored software tests into an ecological accuracy score. Live visual AI success remains unverified; demonstrate only actual consented responses or the real failure/local-check path.
